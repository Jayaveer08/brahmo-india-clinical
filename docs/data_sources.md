## Interactions

Interaction rows were compiled from standard pharmacology mechanisms and guideline safety logic:

- Sulfonylurea plus beta-blocker: masked hypoglycemia.
- Sulfonylurea in CKD: severe/prolonged hypoglycemia risk.
- ACE inhibitor or ARB plus MRA: hyperkalemia.
- Aspirin/P2Y12 plus DOAC/VKA: bleeding and triple therapy risk.
- SGLT2 inhibitor plus loop diuretic: volume depletion/hypotension.
- Pioglitazone in HFrEF: fluid retention and HF worsening.
- Metformin during AKI/diuretic illness: accumulation and lactic acidosis risk.
- Fibrinolytic plus anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy: bleeding protocol risk.
